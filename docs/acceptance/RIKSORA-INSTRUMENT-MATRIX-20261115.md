Notes on the columns: *feed precision* is the number of price digits present in the source and is proven by the verifier. *Feed tick size* is only 10^-digits, a property of the file, not a broker or contract tick size. A fixture in the repository (`backend/execution/research_fixture_v2.py`) uses contract size 100 for XAUUSD and 100,000 otherwise; that is a QA fixture assumption, not a certified specification, and must not be read as one. The only product execution profile with real-looking values is the XAUUSD observed profile; the 15 others have no profile and none was invented. Lot step, minimum and maximum lot, commission, slippage, latency, margin and swap are likewise absent for all 16. Pip conventions are broker/platform conventions and are not in the repository. Account currency is USD; 11 of 16 quote currencies are not USD (JPY x7, GBP, NZD, CAD, CHF), so profit conversion needs a defined, point-in-time conversion rule that does not exist yet.
